GOVERNMENT & PUBLIC SECTOR SECURITY

Architected in Conformity with the National Security Scheme (ENS Media)

The benchmark cybersecurity standard for public administrations and critical infrastructure suppliers in Spain and the EU. VARNATIR implements the control principles prescribed by Royal Decree 311/2022.

  • Strict Logical Isolation Perimeter: Every organization operates within its own dedicated logical isolation boundary. Zero data contamination or cross-tenant access.
  • Backend-Managed End-to-End Encryption: Messages travel encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and rest encrypted (AES-256) with segregated per-organization keys.
  • Immutable Event Traceability: Every moderation intervention, pre-LLM anonymization event, or ephemeral room escalation is permanently recorded in an append-only cryptographic audit log.

FINANCIAL & PRIVACY REGULATIONS

DORA Operational Resilience & GDPR Privacy by Design

How VARNATIR addresses the primary audit hurdles facing enterprise compliance officers, CISOs, and DPOs across Europe.

DORA (EU Regulation 2022/2554)

Mandatory for credit institutions, investment firms, and insurance undertakings across the European Union.

  • ICT Third-Party Risk Management: Eliminates blind dependence on commercial messaging platforms lacking enterprise SLAs and operational safeguards.
  • Business Continuity & Failover: Proprietary conversation state architecture enables routing fallback to alternative channels if a third-party channel experiences downtime.
  • Incident Detection & Reporting: Real-time detection of conversational anomalies, out-of-band policy infractions, or impersonation attempts.

GDPR & LOPDGDD

Zero-exposure protection for personal data of clients, citizens, and employees across messaging channels.

  • Privacy by Design (PbD): Mandatory pre-LLM PII anonymization before any text is evaluated by AI models.*
  • Right to be Forgotten & Erasure: Guaranteed deterministic erasure on enterprise data repositories without relying on third-party carrier retention cycles.
  • Zero Model Training: Corporate payload data is never used to re-train commercial foundational models.

Digital Disconnection & Labor Rights

Enforcing statutory rest periods and digital disconnection rights without employee friction.

  • Automated Operating Hours: Inbound routing stops outside work schedules with polite automated responses; employees never need to manually mute phones.
  • Dual-Identity Coexistence: Personal chats and corporate regulated workflows remain strictly segregated on the same device.
  • Segregated Audit Boundary: The employer governs only the corporate identity line, with absolute zero visibility into personal device activity.
