THE CIBEC 2023 PHD THESIS

The 9 Questions Answered by Scientific Research

The formal theoretical framework diagnosing why conventional telecom and archival approaches fail systematically in instant messaging environments.

Axis 01 · Usage Planes

What truly structures conversation risk?

The strict segregation of three operational planes: personal (BYOD), internal (employee-to-employee), and external (organization-to-client).

Axis 02 · AI Governance

Who governs the AI that drafts and decides?

The technical necessity of isolating context and anonymizing payloads before any request reaches external foundational models.

Axis 03 · Evidentiary Value

What gives a chat legally binding weight?

Append-only cryptographic hash chaining and backend state timestamps capable of withstanding adversarial judicial scrutiny.

Axis 04 · Adoption Friction

Why do proprietary corporate apps fail?

Because forced app installation creates severe friction. Governance must operate at origin while the user stays on WhatsApp.

Axis 05 · Regulatory Alignment

How to reconcile speed with compliance?

By empowering DPOs and CISOs with pre-configured policy rules rather than post-mortem manual audits.

Axis 06 · Boundary Security

Where does telecommunication end and governance start?

At the flight control interception plane, ensuring no uninspected packet reaches consumer messaging layers.
